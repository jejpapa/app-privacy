Privacy Policy

App: Learn Korean: Shadowing TOPIK (Shadowing Korean) · Developer: CIESoft · Last updated: 2026-07-15

Summary

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It works fully offline. There are no user accounts, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics SDKs.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other sensitive device data.

Local Storage

Your learning progress (completed episodes, flashcard know/don't-know marks, and app preferences such as your interface language) is stored only on your device using local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network Use

All learning content (dialogues, audio, vocabulary, Hangul lessons) is bundled with the app and available offline. The app does not transmit your data to any server.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change (for example, if optional online features are added in a future update), this policy will be updated here before such features are released.
